# Smart-Interview-prep-tracker
Tracks progress of your DSA and Interview prep and guides you



A platform that tracks your DSA practice and builds a personalized plan:

stores solved problems
detects weak topics
predicts interview readiness
generates mock interview sets
shows progress over time

Make it unique by adding:

“confidence vs actual performance” tracking
spaced repetition for problem types
voice-based explanation practice
recruiter-style dashboard

Why it stands out:
It solves a problem you actually have, and you can keep improving it over months.
