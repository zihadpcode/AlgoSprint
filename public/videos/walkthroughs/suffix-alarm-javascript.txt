A log scanner watches a stream of lowercase letters that arrive one at a time. It has a

list of forbidden words. After each arriving letter, report whether some forbidden word is a suffix of

everything received so far, meaning the word's letters match the most recent letters of the stream, in order.

Given the forbidden words and the whole stream as a string, return an array with one boolean per

letter.

After "stap" the suffix "tap" is forbidden; after "stape" the suffix "pe" is; "stapep"

ends in nothing forbidden; "stapepy" ends in "y". The first three letters have no match.

After "a" no word ends the stream; after "ab" both "ab" and "b" are suffixes, so one alarm

is enough.

Suffix matching is prefix matching on the reversed strings, and a trie answers prefix questions one character at

a time.

Insert every word reversed into a trie, marking where each word ends. For each arriving letter, walk from

the newest letter toward older ones, following trie edges, and stop at a missing edge, at a marked

node (alarm), or after the longest word's length letters.

A word is a suffix of the stream exactly when its reverse is a prefix of the stream

read backwards. So I build a trie of reversed words, and for each arriving

letter walk it from the newest letter backwards. The walk can never be longer than the longest word,

so each letter costs O(L) and the trie costs O(total letters) memory. Aho-Corasick would

bring the per-letter cost to amortized O(1) by adding failure links, at the price of a more complex

build; with words of at most 20 letters the reversed trie is the practical answer.

For ["tap", "pe", "y"] the paths are p-a-t, e-p and y, each ending at a marked node.

Walking back from 'p' goes p, a, t, landing on the end of "tap": alarm.

'e' then 'p' ends the path of "pe": alarm; the second 'p' finds no edge after 'e'; 'y'

lands on a marked node. Output: false, false, false, true, true, false, true.

O(total word letters) to build; O(L) per arriving letter, where L is the longest word (at most 20),

so O(stream * L) overall. O(total word letters * 26) for the typed-array trie
