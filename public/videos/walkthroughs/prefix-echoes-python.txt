You are given a lowercase string text and a list of lengths. For each length L in lengths,

report how many times the prefix text[0..L) occurs inside text as a contiguous substring. Occurrences may

overlap, and the prefix itself at position 0 counts as one occurrence. Return the counts in the same

order as the lengths.

The prefix "c" occurs at positions 0, 3, 7 and 10, and "cab" at the same

four positions. "cabcab" occurs at positions 0 and 7. "cabcabd" and the whole text each occur once.

"a" occurs 5 times, "aa" starts at positions 0 to 3 so it occurs 4 times, and the

whole text occurs once.

Every end position of an occurrence of a prefix lies on a chain of borders; counting each end

once and pushing the count down the chain gives every prefix's total.

Compute border[i] with the standard failure-function loop. Set echoes[len] = 1 for every len from 1 to n

(the prefix ending at len - 1 is itself an occurrence of that length). For len from n

down to 1, add echoes[len] to echoes[border[len - 1]] when that border is positive. Return echoes[L] for each

query.

An occurrence of the length-L prefix that ends at position e means text[0..L) is a suffix of text[0..e],

i.e. L is a border of the prefix of length e + 1. Borders form a chain through

the border array, so each prefix length contributes 1 to itself and passes its total to its longest

border. Processing lengths from longest to shortest guarantees the total is final when it is pushed. The failure

loop is amortized linear.

border is [0, 1, 2, 3, 4].

From echoes [1, 1, 1, 1, 1], length 5 pushes into 4, giving 2; length 4 pushes 2

into 3, giving 3; and so on to 5 at length 1. The answers for 1, 2 and

5 are 5, 4 and 1.

O(n + queries). O(n)
