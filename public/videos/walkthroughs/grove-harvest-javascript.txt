A grove survey records the yield of every tree in a binary arrangement, given in level order (tree)

with null for a missing child. A yield can be negative where a tree is diseased. A subtree

is a node together with everything below it. A subtree is ordered when every yield in its left

part is strictly smaller than its root's yield, every yield in its right part is strictly larger, and

both parts are themselves ordered; a single node is ordered. Return the largest total yield over all ordered

subtrees, or 0 when every ordered subtree has a negative total, because you may harvest nothing.

The whole tree is not ordered because 5 sits in the right part of 6 but is smaller

than 6. The subtree rooted at 9 (9, 5, 12) is ordered with total 26, beating the subtree

at 3 (3, 2, 4) with total 9.

The whole tree is ordered, but its total is -140 and every single node is negative, so the

best harvest is nothing: 0.

The definition is recursive: a subtree is ordered when both child subtrees are ordered and their extremes

straddle the root, so each call only needs to hand its parent four numbers.

Decode the level order into child index arrays. visit(node) returns { ordered, total, low, high }: a missing

child is ordered with total 0, low = +infinity and high = -infinity. A node is ordered when

both children are, left.high < value and right.low > value; then its total is left.total + right.total +

value, its low is min(left.low, value) and its high is max(right.high, value). Record the best total whenever a

node is ordered, starting from 0.

A subtree is ordered when its left subtree is ordered with a maximum below the root, its right

subtree is ordered with a minimum above the root, and the root lies strictly between

them. So each subtree summarises itself as (ordered, total, min, max) and the parent combines two summaries in

O(1). That is a natural post-order recursion and runs in O(n) time. The call stack

is as deep as the tree is tall, so a degenerate tree of thousands of levels needs O(n)

stack; the iterative reverse sweep trades the recursion for an index loop over the level order.

Nodes 2, 4, 5 and 12 are ordered singles with totals equal to their yields.

Node 9 has left max 5 < 9 and right min 12 > 9, total 26. Node 3

has total 9. Node 6 sees left max 4 < 6 but right min 5 < 6, so

it is not ordered.

The best total among ordered nodes is 26.

O(n) for n non-null nodes (the serialized length is at most 2n). O(n) for the decoded child arrays,

plus a recursion stack as deep as the tree is tall, which reaches O(n) on a chain
